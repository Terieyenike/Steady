# Steady — 60-second Moonshot demo

Read only the **Say** lines: about 150 words, six beats of roughly ten seconds. Fallbacks replace the live action; do not read them in addition.

The app also supports a separate manual buddy-file exchange, outside this 60-second example-feedback demo.

**Before starting:** open the production app online, reset with confirmation, and save the example contact-form commitment. Have these two short entries ready to paste: evidence, “Created three labeled form inputs”; reflection, “Labels connect using for and id.” Rehearse the clicks with a stopwatch. No screenshots or recording are assumed available.

## 1 · 0–10 seconds — The person and their problem

**Say:** “Imagine Ada, an aspiring developer in Lagos learning alone. She starts lessons with good intentions, but struggles to return. Steady is built around this learner.”

**Show:** the saved contact-form goal and milestone.

**Fallback:** “Ada is an illustrative learner, not someone we have already tested with.”

## 2 · 10–20 seconds — The moment it hurts

**Say:** “She misses a study session. When she returns, she has no finished form and no clear next step. Starting again feels harder than it should.”

**Show:** the milestone’s completion condition; start the focus session.

**Fallback:** “The problem we want to test is that difficult moment of returning to unfinished learning.”

## 3 · 20–30 seconds — What I show on screen

**Say:** “Here’s her commitment: build a contact form. She focuses, then records what she made and learned. I’m using the timer’s clearly marked demo shortcut today.”

**Show:** select **Finish session for demo**, paste the evidence and reflection, then **Save milestone & check in**.

**Fallback:** “The working path is commitment, focus, recorded work, and reflection; the timer alone never completes a milestone.”

## 4 · 30–40 seconds — The wow moment

**Say:** “This buddy response is simulated. I choose ‘Add accessible error messages.’ Now her completed work and next step are visible. Refresh, and both stay here.”

**Show:** open simulated feedback, commit to the next step, then refresh. Point to the completed count and next milestone.

**Fallback:** “Saved work and the next milestone remain on this browser; there is no live buddy message.”

## 5 · 40–50 seconds — How Codex helped

**Say:** “I chose the scope. Codex generated the app and tests. We checked the full journey, saved progress, timer recovery, and keyboard use on small screens.”

**Show:** keep the progress summary visible; no window switching needed.

**Fallback:** “Codex helped build and test this prototype; today’s feedback is fixed example text, not AI.”

## 6 · 50–60 seconds — What is next

**Say:** “Next, I’ll test this with real learners: does it help them return? Easier buddy sharing comes later. Today, it’s one working journey on one browser.”

**Show:** finish on the next action and browser-local privacy note.

**Fallback:** “Learner testing and easier sharing are next steps; real feedback currently requires exchanging files manually.”
