# Steady — turn study sessions into visible progress

**Build-week product brief · 13 October 2026**

## Who and what problem
**Illustrative persona, to validate:** Ada is an aspiring software developer in Lagos learning HTML and CSS independently alongside other commitments. She needs an achievable plan and someone to return to after each study session.

**Concrete example — assumed, not a research finding:** Ada watches a lesson on forms, misses her next planned session, and returns unsure where to restart. She has no finished form to show and nobody expecting a check-in. Our hypothesis is that a small practical commitment, visible evidence, and supportive accountability will help her resume. We have not established that this improves course completion.

## What the product does
Steady gives Ada one learning goal, a small practical milestone, and a Pomodoro focus session. After studying, she records what she built, adds a work link or short text evidence, and checks in with an accountability buddy. Feedback leads to one achievable next milestone. Saved progress survives refresh on the same browser.

The prototype demonstrates one learner journey with a **clearly labeled simulated buddy response**, plus a separate manual file exchange for real written feedback. Learners can plan several milestones and keep a focus-session history. It is browser-local: learners share check-in and response files themselves; the app does not deliver messages. A lightweight “study room” is a session screen showing the commitment and timer, without live presence or calls.

## Why now
The user-provided showcase is Moonshot by TechCabal, Lagos, **28–29 October 2026**, with a short live demo. One build week is an opportunity to test this accountability loop with a learner before expanding it. The need and proposed benefit remain assumptions until tested.

## The one wow moment
Ada finishes a focus session, submits evidence of her working form, opens a labeled example buddy response, and commits to “add accessible error messages.” Her dashboard immediately shows the completed milestone and next action; refreshing preserves both. A visible demo-only timer shortcut keeps this sequence under three minutes.

## What we will not build
Real-time rooms, video conferencing, live peer messaging or matching, authentication, cloud sync, course integrations, a marketplace, leaderboards, automated competence scores, AI tutoring, and push notifications. We will not claim verified buddy identities, verified learning, or improved completion rates.
